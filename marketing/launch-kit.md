## Positioning

EU TED Procurement Intelligence turns official Tenders Electronic Daily notices into
CRM-ready lead rows. It is built for sales teams, bid teams, consultants, and market
intelligence teams that need buyer, country, CPV, value, dates, stage, and official
source links without maintaining TED ingestion code.

## Short Pitch

Find EU public procurement leads from official TED data. Filter by country, CPV,
and publication date, then export normalized rows with buyer, value, stage, dates,
and official source links.

## Long Pitch

EU TED Procurement Intelligence converts official EU Tenders Electronic Daily data
into normalized, privacy-safe procurement lead rows. It is useful for companies
selling into public sector markets, bid teams watching opportunities, consultants
tracking demand, and analysts building BI dashboards from procurement notices.

The Actor returns one row per notice with buyer identity, buyer country, CPV,
estimated value, procurement stage, dates, official source URL, and quality signals.
Outputs are ready for CRM import, spreadsheets, BI tools, pandas, and DuckDB.
